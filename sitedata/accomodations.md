### Accomodations

INLG 2026 does not have an official hotel. However, you can use [this search facility](https://lennonservices.nl/congress/module/80eec27e-2fd0-4366-adea-8e8e9e44f20c/hotel?thema_id=76) to find and book a hotel for your stay. This is a service provided for INLG courtesy of [Hotel Service Utrecht](https://www.hotelserviceutrecht.com/). You are not obliged to use this service.

We also invite you to consider accommodations in the vicinity of Utrecht (Zeist, Amersfoort), as these tend to have lower average rates and are largely reachable by public transport.
